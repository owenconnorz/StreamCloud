---
name: Expanded movie-home cards
description: Product decision for focused home-card artwork and trailer placement.
---

Focused cards on the Movies home screen use static artwork with the existing title/logo treatment instead of trailer playback. Trailer previews remain on detail screens.

**Why:** The user requested thumbnail-style expanded home cards while keeping trailers available on detail pages.

**How to apply:** When changing focused TMDB, Stremio, or CloudStream cards on Movies home, use backdrop or poster artwork with the available title logo. Keep detail-screen trailer previews separate.
